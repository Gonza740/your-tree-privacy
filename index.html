Your Tree · Android Game

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters while you play and grow.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Your Tree (the “Game,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use our Android mobile game. If you have questions, contact us at davidgonzabjj@gmail.com.

1. Information you provide

Your Tree does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, phone number, or email address to play. We do not intentionally request personal information through the Game.

If you email us for support, we will receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond to you and handle your request.

2. Advertising and information collected by third parties

The Game displays third-party advertisements. The advertising services integrated into the Game may automatically collect or process certain information when an advertisement is requested, displayed, or interacted with. Depending on the advertising provider, your device settings, consent choices, and applicable law, this information may include:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from the IP address;
  • Device or advertising identifiers, where available and permitted;
  • Device information, operating system details, and app-related technical information;
  • Ad views, clicks, interactions, and other advertising-related activity; and
  • Diagnostic and performance information used to maintain security, detect fraud, or improve ad delivery.

Advertising providers may use these details to deliver and measure advertisements, limit repeated ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where permitted and subject to applicable consent requirements, personalize advertising. These providers may act as independent data controllers or processors under their own policies. We do not directly receive or control all information collected by their SDKs.

Advertising features generally require an Internet connection. The core gameplay can be used offline, but ads may be unavailable without connectivity.

3. Analytics

We do not currently integrate Firebase Analytics or a separate first-party analytics service into Your Tree. However, third-party advertising providers may collect ad-related interaction and diagnostic information as described above, including for ad measurement and reporting.

4. Why information is used

Information handled through the Game or by our service providers may be used to display and measure ads, maintain the Game and advertising services, troubleshoot issues, detect and prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and respond to support requests. Advertising personalization is subject to available choices and applicable legal requirements.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell contact information that players send directly to us. Information may be transmitted to advertising technology providers through the advertising services included in the Game. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect rights, security, and users. The exact providers and their processing practices depend on the SDKs included in the released version of the Game.

6. Local game data

The Game may store gameplay preferences or progress locally on your device so that your experience can continue between sessions. Local data is not the same as information transmitted by advertising providers. Local game data can generally be removed by clearing the Game's storage or uninstalling it, although uninstalling does not automatically delete data that a third party previously collected.

7. Children's privacy

We do not intentionally ask children to submit personal information through the Game. If the Game is made available to children, we will apply the age-appropriate advertising and privacy measures required by Google Play policies and applicable law, including restrictions on personalized ads where required. Parents or guardians who have concerns can contact us using the email address below.

8. Your privacy choices

You may be able to reset or delete your Android advertising ID and adjust advertising privacy settings in your device's settings. Where legally required, advertising consent and privacy choices will be provided through the relevant consent mechanism. You may also play the Game offline, though advertisements and certain ad-supported functionality may not be available. You can contact us to ask about personal information associated with a support request or to request its deletion, subject to applicable law.

9. Data retention and deletion

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage related requests, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. You can request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing us. Gameplay data stored only on your device can usually be deleted through Android app-storage controls or by uninstalling the Game. Advertising partners determine retention periods for data they process under their respective policies and applicable law; requests concerning their processing may need to be directed to those providers.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information within our control. Third-party advertising providers are responsible for implementing safeguards for information processed through their services. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International processing

Advertising providers may process data on servers located outside your country of residence. Such processing is governed by applicable laws and the providers' privacy practices.

12. External links and advertisements

Advertisements may lead to third-party websites, apps, or services that are not operated by us. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We encourage you to review them before providing any information.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the Game, advertising providers, legal requirements, or privacy practices. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. If a change requires additional notice or consent under applicable law, we will take the necessary steps.

14. Contact us

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns regarding Your Tree, contact the developer at davidgonzabjj@gmail.com.
